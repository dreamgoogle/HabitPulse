Delete your HabitPulse account

Play Store requirement ke mutabik — account aur data delete karne ka tareeka.

Ye wapas nahi laya ja sakta. Delete karne par aapke saare habits, daily logs aur profile hamesha ke liye mit jaate hain.

Tareeka 1 — App ke andar se (sabse tez)

  1. HabitPulse app kholiye
  2. Upar daayein taraf apne profile par tap kijiye
  3. Danger Zone tak scroll kijiye
  4. Delete My Account & All Data dabaiye
  5. Confirm karne ke liye DELETE type kijiye

Tareeka 2 — Email se

Agar aap app access nahi kar pa rahe, to apne registered email address se ravitripathi.1310@gmail.com par likhiye, subject: "Delete my HabitPulse account".

Hum aapki request 30 din ke andar process kar denge aur confirmation bhejenge.

Kya-kya delete hota hai

  • Aapke saare habits (naam, category, icon, goals)
  • Aapke saare daily check-in logs — poora history
  • Aapki profile (naam, email, photo URL)
  • Aapka authentication account

Kya nahi hatta

  • Jo Google Sheets aap pehle export kar chuke hain — wo aapki apni Drive mein hain aur unka ownership aapka hai. Unhe aapko khud delete karna hoga.
  • Jo CSV files aapne download ki hain — wo aapke device par hain.
  • Aggregated, anonymous usage counts jisme aapki koi pehchaan nahi hoti.
