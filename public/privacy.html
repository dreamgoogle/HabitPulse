2. Hum kaun sa data collect karte hain

  • App activity — aapke banaye gaye habits (naam, category, icon, weekly goal) aur aapke daily check-in records (kaunsa habit kis din complete hua).

Hum aapka data advertising ke liye use nahi karte, aur na hi kisi ko bechte hain.

Ye permission app ko sirf unhi files tak access deta hai jo app khud banata hai — aapki baaki Google Drive hum dekh hi nahi sakte. Bani hui sheet aapki apni Drive mein rehti hai aur uska ownership aapka hota hai.
